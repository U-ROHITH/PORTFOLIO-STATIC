From first conversation to launch

Every BurntStack project moves through three stages. Decisions and responsibilities are written down at each one.

  1. 1 Define the requirement We review the business, its users, current systems, constraints and the outcome the product has to support. You get a written understanding of the problem and a practical next step.
  2. 2 Approve the scope Features, integrations, responsibilities and delivery stages are documented before any development starts. You get a defined scope, a delivery plan and a quotation to approve.
  3. 3 Build and release We design, develop, test and deploy the agreed product, then hand over everything your team needs. You get a working release with access, documentation and a support plan.
